I’m Jay Chaudhary: MBA, IIM Raipur, with a focus on product management. With enterprise banking experience at Oracle Financial Services Software and hands-on product work at Myntra, I connect user problems with practical technical solutions.

My journey began in computer science and enterprise banking technology. At Oracle, I worked close to the systems and stakeholders behind banking operations. My product internship at Myntra brought that technical foundation into sourcing decisions and everyday designer workflows.
